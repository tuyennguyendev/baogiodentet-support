Settings & countdown

Where are settings?

Open the menu at the top right of the home screen, then choose Settings. You can also reach Add event, Lunar calendar and Share from this menu.

Are settings saved automatically?

Yes. Each change in Settings is applied and saved immediately. Return to the home screen without pressing Done. When creating or editing an event, use Save event (or Save for a built-in holiday).

How do I change the background or use my own photo?

Go to Settings → Background to choose one of six Tet backgrounds or select Your photo. Your selected photo is copied into app storage for use as a background; the app does not upload it to a server.

Can I change the counting format and text size?

In Settings → Counting, choose By day, By week or By month. Months use 30-day units rather than calendar months. Text size offers Small, Medium and Large.

How do I show hours, minutes and seconds?

Turn on Show hours and minutes in Settings to reveal Show seconds. With less than a day remaining, the countdown automatically switches to hours and minutes. In the final minute, it focuses on seconds and temporarily hides the menu.

Where did Text effects, Text position and Revert go?

The redesigned interface uses a fixed text layout with three text sizes. Settings save immediately, so there is no Revert button or per-tab save step.

What happens at Lunar New Year?

If the app is open at the turn of the year, the countdown ends with fireworks. Tap to skip the show. During the first three days of Tet, the screen shows the day of Tet and a Watch fireworks button. Afterwards, it counts down to the next Tet.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose Tiếng Việt or English. This choice is also sent to the device widget. The language switch on this website only changes the support pages.

Events & reminders

How do I add a birthday or anniversary?

Open the menu → Add event. Enter a name, select Lunar or Solar and choose a date. Optionally enable Repeat yearly, Show on the home screen and Remind me, then tap Save event.

Where do I manage holidays and personal events?

Go to Settings → Event list. General contains built-in holidays and Personal contains your own events. Toggle milestones, or edit and delete personal events. Built-in holiday names and dates are fixed. Holiday dates do not represent an official annual leave or compensatory day-off schedule.

How do I change the event display?

Go to Settings → Event style and choose Glass card, Glass pills or Swipe card. Scroll or swipe the event band to see more milestones; tap one to open its details.

How do I set an event reminder?

Open an event, turn on Remind me and allow notifications when prompted. Choose the event day or a number of days beforehand, select a time and save the event. Keep Show on the home screen enabled: disabled milestones are not scheduled for reminders.

Why did a reminder not arrive?

Check that the event is saved, enabled and has a reminder time in the future. Check notification permission in system settings. On Android, also review battery restrictions using the guidance in the event editor. Reopen the app after changing permissions or time zones to refresh reminders. Delivery timing can be affected by the operating system.

Does a yearly lunar event keep the same solar date?

No. The app calculates the corresponding solar date for each year. A personal event on lunar day 30 skips years where that month has only 29 days and looks for the next valid occurrence; it does not silently move your event to day 29.

Lunar calendar & sharing

Where can I view and convert lunar and solar dates?

Open the menu → Lunar calendar. Browse months and select a date for its lunar/solar details, traditional cyclical names and auspicious hours. Use Choose date to look up a lunar or solar date, or Today to return to the current date.

How do I share a countdown image?

Open the menu → Share, review the preview and tap Share to choose a destination app. The image contains your background and Tet countdown, without the personal event band. If you use your own background photo, it will appear in the shared image.

Where is “What is Tet”?

Go to Settings → About to read seven chapters about Tet. Reading progress is stored on your device so you can continue or start again.

Widgets & data

How do I add an iPhone or Android widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen, open the widget list and choose Bao giờ đến Tết. On iPhone, choose a size and add it; on Android, drag the widget into place. See the Apple or Google instructions if your device uses different menus.

Which widget types are supported?

iOS offers small and medium Home Screen widgets and circular and rectangular Lock Screen widgets. Android offers a Home Screen widget. Widgets receive the app language and background; the background is blurred for readability, while Lock Screen widgets use system styling. The macOS app has no separate native widget.

What if an old widget is blank after updating?

Open the app once, remove the old widget and add the current widget from the picker. The widget set changed with the redesign. If the background or language has not changed yet, allow time for the system to refresh it; widgets do not run continuously like the app screen.

Do I need an account or Internet access?

No account is required. The countdown, events, lunar calendar and Tet chapters use on-device data. Sending support email or sharing through another app may require Internet access.

Does my data sync to another device?

The app has no account sync or app-managed cloud backup. Events, reminders, settings, background photos and reading progress are stored on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove this data; restoration depends on any operating-system backup. See the Privacy Policy.
